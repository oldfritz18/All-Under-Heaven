## M5–M7: Playable Emperor frontline warfare, Dragon autonomy & earned China unification

## Approved owner requirement — 16 November 2026

**Codex implementation directive:** Preserve the established Part II arc: lawful **Emperor of Shu enthronement → optional 0–5 individually earned Five Dragon Generals → escalating Wei/Wu/Shu campaigns and relationships → earned China unification OR valid alternative endings → Epilogue**. The Emperor **must retain a substantively playable direct battlefield/warrior-commander route** during relevant major campaigns, even after enthronement. He must not be a palace-only spectator whose Dragon Generals monopolize all decisive action.

### Authoritative sources and scope
- `AGENTS.md`, `docs/START_HERE.md`, `GOAL_MODE_PROMPT.md`, `GAME/ACTIVE_BUILD.txt` and `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
- `ROADMAP/06_EMPEROR_DRAGONS_UNIFICATION_ARC.md`, especially section 5 "Partisipasi langsung protagonis di medan perang"; `ROADMAP/01_TWO_PART_190_250_v1.3.md`, `ROADMAP/05_M0_M11_DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP_v1.0.md`.
- Existing **Issue #1** and `DESIGN/APPROVED_EPIC_ELEVATIONS_ISSUE_1.md` govern the earned General/Sixth Tiger, enthronement and personal Dragon ceremonies. **Do not duplicate or prematurely close Issue #1**. This issue covers their **long-term wartime payoff, direct player frontline agency, and the earned unification arc through M5–M7**.

### Mandatory playable design
1. **Emperor fights and commands directly.** In every major relevant M5–M7 campaign, if actual chronology, geography, authority, health and unit availability make it plausible, allow the player to choose an organic direct expedition/frontline route. The Emperor may make strategic and tactical decisions, command formations, maneuver with real troops and personally fight/duel only where physically and narratively legitimate. Important clashes must be playable, not just described as events the player supposedly led.
2. **No forced frontline in every battle.** Delegation to autonomous Dragons, court governance, defense, and diplomatic outcomes remain meaningful alternative paths with their own consequences. If the player stays at court, do not record a frontline meeting or combat achievement.
3. **Battle scene chain:** legitimate council/order → actual troop muster/command provenance → travel/deployment → named formation, officer/soldier context, visible enemies, objective, terrain/supply/weather and limited information → tactical options/opponent countermeasures → credible success/withdrawal/defeat/injury/capture/death risks → casualties, recovery and public/private aftermath → callbacks. Avoid instant one-choice victories and unearned armies/honours.
4. **NPC autonomy.** Each appointed Dragon has distinct personality, goals, doctrine, merit, consent and real command; can advise, dissent, independently act, succeed, err, refuse, depart or die as actual world state permits. Grand General seat does not create troops. Dragons cannot eclipse the protagonist's decisive choices, and the Emperor cannot eclipse all other commanders or override historical facts without earned alternate history.
5. **Novel-length emotional build-up.** The Shu enthronement is a central climax, each Dragon appointment a separate personal payoff (continuing into later campaigns), XIV–XVII develop tensions/bonds/tragedy/quiet domestic scenes and political costs, and XVIII–XIX converge in a major *played* campaign/settlement and earned public recognition of unification. No automatic reunification on Shu coronation or a mandatory happy ending. Wu remains independent until genuinely settled.
6. **Persistence & compatibility.** Story events alone update Military, Civic, People, Character and Chronicle (plus relevant relationship/possession/ending state). Preserve existing scene IDs, choices, portraits, saves and 8 ending families. Separate lawful Emperor of Shu status from eventual all-China sovereign/unification proof; audit legacy `auh79Emperor` and royal predicates before remapping.
7. **Timeline & milestone integration.** Keep Chapter XII–XIX and Epilogue, M4→M7 authoring order, and M8–M11 release gates. Preserve the current active HTML and do not recreate game. Continue nearer-term M3-B then M4 without delaying all progress for a distant chapter design debate.

- No unearned combat provenance or knowledge for stay-behind, old-save, dead/unavailable NPC, impossible location/year or unit without rightful assignment.
- Different Dragon configurations 0–5, refusals/vacancies, independently acting companions and court/household callbacks, without forced five-person cutscene.
- Wei and Wu settlement proof checked separately, including unification route plus non-unification routes; final ceremony is not just a title flag.
- QA under mobile and desktop UI, handler/state, idempotency, save/export/import/legacy parity; document exact hash, tested paths, limitations and remaining device `file://` gate in `docs/PROGRESS.md` and specific QA receipts.
