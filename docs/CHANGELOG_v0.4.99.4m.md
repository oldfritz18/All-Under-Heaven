## Editorial/gameplay changes

1. **The War Beyond One Tent:** extended the existing coalition scene with inter-contingent authority, supply-road friction, and natural humour between Xu Wen and his fellow soldiers.
2. **Seals Without Grain:** made the road evidence concrete (broken cart, road barrier, conflicting tally), clarified which actions constitute directly meeting Zhang Miao and Qiao Mao, and retained three distinct choices.
3. **Those Whose Names Remain:** enriched the relief-wagon sequence with the lived experience of displaced families and wounded soldiers; Bao Xin and Liu Dai remain autonomous commanders rather than automatic allies.
4. **Before a Column Departs:** expanded the route-tablet scene and Cao Cao's presence without giving the protagonist access to private plans or knowledge of the ford after he leaves.
5. **The Names Behind the Seals:** added nine distinguishable outcome reflections across the optional paths. The narrative now separates direct audiences, indirect messages and non-intervention.
6. **Chapter II conditional recall:** added a retrospective paragraph to existing `ch2_05`, strictly gated by the **actual visited scene** and the corresponding prior choice. A flag alone cannot create a historic audience or retrospective memory in an imported save.
