# All Under Heaven — Battlefield Position Awareness v1.0

**Approved by player:** 14 November 2026

This decision complements `ALL_UNDER_HEAVEN_COMBAT_ROLE_LOCK_v1.0.md` and applies to **every battle** in Parts I and II.

## Mandatory narrative requirements

- The protagonist must know the **branch** (Infantry/Cavalry only), **formation/rank** (front, center, rear, left/right wing, reserve, or legitimate cavalry position), **unit commander**, **nearby friend and foe**, and **tactical objective**.
- Every reposition must be caused by a witnessed order, player action, or battlefield circumstance. Never teleport the protagonist between formations.
- The viewpoint is limited to what the character can see, hear or credibly learn. A famous general's action at another sector cannot become firsthand detail without proximity or a later report.
- Support personnel are NPCs. Fighting to secure the passage for wounded men is permissible, but hero cannot be assigned to medical, logistics, administrative or noncombat courier roles.
- Record service branch and current or last battlefield position in the **Military UI** only if an actual assignment/action occurred. **No inference solely from year, chapter, rank or generic flags**.
- Preserve canonical ROTK characters' notable deeds; protagonist is a participant in the army, not a spectator or a replacement for the novel's heroes.
- Chronicle and People must reflect verified actions only. Ranks, medals, troop command and cavalry transfers require explicit authorized scenes.
- Finish/withdraw from battle by changing battlefield position status to **last witnessed**, not leaving an active position forever.
